# PEAK HOUR v2 - balance simulation report

Generated by `tests/sim_balance.py` from `data/balance.json`.
Seeds: **240**, hours: **9**, dt: **0.25 s**, send quality q=**0.85**, attack quality q=**0.85**, protocol: **TCP**.

## Model assumptions

- Spawn: file budget from `hours[].files` (+ carry), spawn only during 75% of the hour (225 s), interval 225/budget with +/-20% jitter. A full buffer freezes spawns without burning the budget.
- Types: `hours[].type_weights` only; zero weight never spawns.
- Unsent files persist across hours (the folder is not cleared); only files consumed by a successful send disappear. Unspawned budget is carried separately, capped at 20 files/hour.
- Requests: `request_cap` and `active_requests` from `hours[]`; interval `hours[].send_interval`; count from `request_mix`; a request reserves available files at spawn and does not consume request budget when no files exist.
- Player: competent, attaches after 1.5 s + 1 s/file, plays a no-timer send minigame for 7 + 1.5*tier seconds. The minigame pauses packet spawn/loading but not other request timers (production order).
- Timers: request `30 + 5*timer_level` (cap 55), packet 20 s. Timers pause in the player's own minigame and in the out-slot queue; payout uses `time_left` snapshotted at send start.
- Transfers: `base_time_sec * 0.92^speed_level * max(1, load/capacity)`; slots are shared by receive and send; files are consumed on successful send.
- Payout: exact `round(base_sum * speed * quality * multi * protocol * streak * money_mult)`; recv_fee is added on receive and is not multiplied.
- Folder: `grid.folder_dims[folder_level]`, 2 free folders at start, up to `constants.folders_max` (6) free folders created in prep. The spec allows up to 12; using 6 is the conservative reading and still makes `lot_folder` nearly worthless. Fragmentation is not modelled; cell capacity/occupancy is.
- Shop: 3 weighted offers after each hour, `last_offer` x0.5, pity x1.5, no rerolls (locks are irrelevant), at least one affordable offer; buy the best affordable predicted-ROI lot while ROI >= 0.5 before H9, and any positive ROI in the final prep (there is no shop after H9).
- ROI proxy: `money` = 5% of projected remaining income; `slots`/`speed`/`channel`/`timer`/`folder`/`sla`/`shield` use measured bottlenecks from the previous hour. It is deterministic, not an oracle.

## Tuning recommendations (not applied)

- No blockers: every hour mean is inside +/-20% and every analytical maximum cap is at least its target.
### Weak lots

These lots are bought only by the final-prep money dump (no shop after H9), so their spend is not evidence of value.

- `lot_shield` (shield_charge): mean level 1.43, ROI 0.12. q=0.85 clears every attack threshold, so auto-blocks only add 50% rewards. Lower the price or raise attack rewards/fail costs.
- `lot_folder` (folder_cells): mean level 1.60, ROI 0.14. Cap free folders or make the grid the binding resource; with 2-6 free folders the lot has almost no marginal value.
- `lot_sla` (sla_cap): mean level 1.46, ROI 0.06. SLA rarely binds for a competent player (2/240 defeats). Either lower the price/raise the effect or make timeouts hurt more.

### Best ROI is not the money lot

- Best ROI proxy is `lot_channel` (channel_capacity) at **8.94**, spend share 13.4%. `lot_money` is not the primary lever in this model, so a money-price change alone will not fix the tree coverage; check channel/slot/speed prices too.
- `lot_money` ROI is **1.05** (spend share 25.9%). It is a good buy but not front-loaded enough to justify a new price curve from this run; if you want one anyway, [120, 170, 230, 300, 390, 500, 640, 800] is the mild version (total 3 150 $).
